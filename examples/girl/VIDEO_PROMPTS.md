# 《总说「你说得对」的女孩》 The Girl Who Said "You're Right" · image-to-video prompts

**17 clips**, one per start frame in `start_frames/`. The file names are the clip names. Name each clip after its start frame (for example `07_harbor.mp4`).

**Settings for every clip:**
- 16:9, 10 s, 1080p or higher (MiniMax H3 or Hailuo).
- Use the matching `.jpg` as the **first frame**.

**Global rules:**
- The camera moves slowly.
- Characters stay on-model: same face, hair, outfit and colours.
- Keep the painted anime-illustration texture.

The cut runs on the 112 BPM track used for *正在输入* (3:18). Only the first 5–8 s of most clips will be used, so put the important motion early.
